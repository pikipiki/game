## Heroes of Might and Magic III artwork and audio

`src/assets/h3/*.png`, and their embedded copies in `dist/index.html` / `JOUER.html`, were converted from the user-supplied Heroes III CD archives h3sprite.lod and H3BITMAP.LOD. `src/assets/audio/*.mp3` and `*.wav` were extracted from the user-supplied installer music files and Heroes3.snd. Selected music: GRASS.MP3, CstleTown.mp3, COMBAT01.MP3. Selected effects: BUTTON, AAGLATTK, LIGHTBLT, AAGLMOVE. The catapult impact currently reuses AAGLATTK.

Original artwork and audio: New World Computing / The 3DO Company; Heroes and Might and Magic trademarks belong to their respective rights holders, including Ubisoft. VCMI's public documentation and configuration were consulted for binary format facts and native canvas coordinates. No VCMI engine source code was incorporated. https://github.com/vcmi/vcmi and https://vcmi.eu/modders/File_Formats/

Unshield 1.6.2 (https://github.com/twogood/unshield, MIT) was used locally to extract the InstallShield CAB. Its executable and sources are not included in the game archive.
